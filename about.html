About

What PWTDS really is

A passive Wi-Fi security scanner that tells everyday people whether the network they're on is safe to browse, log in, shop, or bank on.

Passive reads only your own connection 9 checks run in seconds 100% local nothing is uploaded

How it works

You press Scan this network. PWTDS reads facts about the Wi-Fi you're connected to — its encryption, the DNS it hands out, the gateway, your device's exposure, and more — and runs nine checks. It then gives a plain verdict and a "what you can and can't do here" report.

1

Connect

Join the public Wi-Fi as normal — a café, campus, or free hotspot.

2

Scan

3

Get a safe / careful / avoid verdict for real activities.

What it does
  • Reads facts your operating system already exposes
  • Checks the Wi-Fi you're connected to — and only yours
  • Runs ordinary DNS and HTTPS checks against public servers
  • Reports in plain language anyone can act on
What it doesn't do
  • No port scans or probing of other people's devices
  • No deauthentication, key cracking or packet injection
  • No credential capture — nothing personal is stored
  • No offensive testing of networks you don't own

Honest limits

A green result means no problems were found by these checks — not an absolute guarantee. When a check can't be verified, PWTDS says "Can't tell" rather than assuming safe. The assistant explains results but the scan report itself is always the source of truth.

The assistant

The "Ask" button opens a helper that explains your scan in plain language. With a free AI key (see the README) it becomes a full chatbot; without one it uses built-in answers. Either way it's grounded in your real scan and won't tell you something is safe that the scan flagged.
